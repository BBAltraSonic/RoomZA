# Product

## Register
product

## Users
High-intent renters in the South African rental market, and landlords needing structured manual control over their applicants.

## Product Purpose
Map-first rental discovery and structured application management. Optimizes for conversion quality and controlled renter supply rather than infinite inventory size.

## Brand Personality
Focused, structured, frictionless, deliberate.

## Anti-references
Property24, Private Property. Noisy, unstructured descriptions, uninhibited messaging, and anonymous chat.

## Design Principles
- Map never disappears.
- No long text blocks.
- Structured data (bento grids) over descriptive text.
- Friction only where it improves lead quality.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Structured layouts for high legibility and clear visual contrast across the map and discovery components. Full keyboard navigability in applicant dashboards.
