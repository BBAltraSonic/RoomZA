# Product

## Register
product

## Users
People discovering homes, local services, shopping specials, places, and events in South Africa. Every ordinary account can both consume and list; there is no consumer/lister mode switch.

## Product Purpose
Map-first local discovery with one account for saved listings, enquiries, applications, and listing management. Category review and publication rules remain independent of ordinary account capabilities.

## Brand Personality
Focused, structured, frictionless, deliberate.

## Anti-references
Property24, Private Property. Noisy, unstructured descriptions, uninhibited messaging, and anonymous chat.

## Design Principles
- Map never disappears.
- No long text blocks.
- Structured data (bento grids) over descriptive text.
- Friction only where it improves lead quality.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Structured layouts for high legibility and clear visual contrast across the map and discovery components. Full keyboard navigability in applicant dashboards.
