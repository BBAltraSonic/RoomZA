South African place data

18 252 source-backed named-area records across all nine provinces. These names and coordinates come from GeoNames and © OpenStreetMap contributors. Snapshot downloaded on 11 October 2026.

The catalogue includes cities, towns, suburbs, townships, villages and rural settlements. Open data has gaps. 621 records with conflicting province geometry and 3046 possible cross-source duplicates are held for sourced correction or review.

Where a source supplies an extent, Pinpoint uses it. Other locations open a nearby viewing area approximately 10km across. Zoom to adjust your search area; a viewing area is not an official suburb boundary. A valid location can have no published Pinpoint listings.
