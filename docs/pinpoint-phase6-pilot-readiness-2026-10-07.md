## Proposed pilot acceptance and stop criteria

**All numerical thresholds below are proposals until BB accepts them.** They are decision-ready starting values, not observed results, commitments or substitutes for mandatory pre-launch evidence. BB must also accept cohort size, staffed hours, observation window, metric source and who acts. Proposed observation: a seven-day restricted cohort, initially at least 20 explicitly consented discovery/action attempts; assess API error rate only after 100 eligible requests. All sample sizes and duration are proposals. Low volume remains insufficient evidence, not a pass.

| Measure | Proposed acceptance | Proposed stop/escalation trigger | Measurement / accountable role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Approval turnaround | Complete eligible submissions reviewed within 24 staffed hours; incomplete submissions receive a correction reason | Oldest complete item exceeds 48 staffed hours: halt new intake/expansion and resolve capacity; no shortcut approvals | Received-complete → decision timestamps; reviewer/Operations unassigned |
| Stale/incorrect content | Triage within 2 staffed hours; affected publication corrected or unpublished within 4 staffed hours | Confirmed wrong venue/authority/expired actionable content: contain affected publication immediately; suspend affected cohort if no authorized containment is available | Restricted report/decision/public recheck timestamps; reviewer/support unassigned |
| Successful discovery/actions | At least 95% of 20+ eligible attempts complete selected-place discovery and correct approved action destination | Below 90% over 20 attempts, or three consecutive reproducible critical-path failures: stop affected cohort and investigate | Numerator/denominator, category/place/view/client, error class; Operations unassigned. Intent is not a visit/booking |
| Application errors | Under 1% unexpected server failures across 100+ eligible requests; no untriaged repeating critical error | At least 5% over 100 requests, or three consecutive genuine critical-path failures: stop affected cohort | Sanitized request outcomes/route/window; Operations/technical owner unassigned. Exclude only documented expected denials; count retries separately |
| Privacy/authorization | Zero cross-owner leaks, unauthorized writes/publication or MFA bypass | Any confirmed instance immediately stops affected pilot scope; all scope stops if containment is uncertain | Current native-cookie/API/private-byte denial evidence and restricted incident log; incident lead/BB |
| Recovery | Provisional legacy objectives: database RPO ≤24h; DB RTO ≤4h; private-document recovery ≤24h, subject to BB acceptance | Missing/inconsistent backup, failed restore/checksum/private-access proof or objective exceeded: no start/reopen; incident lead escalates to BB for recovery decision | Consistent cutoff + newest verified recoverable point; timed full restore including objects/access; Operations unassigned |
