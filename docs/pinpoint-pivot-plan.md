## Direction and scope

Build a useful local discovery front door around the existing property app. The location selector covers South Africa and is shared across discovery categories; Rondebosch in the reference is an example selection, not a fixed launch location. Shopping Offers, Tourism and Events provide reasons to return; Property retains its established discovery and transaction journeys. The user selected **a small pilot followed by evidence-led expansion**, then clarified the universal location selector, launch categories and publishing ownership. The supplied proposal and screenshot are reference material; this document is a plan, not an implementation or production change.

Release 1: Shopping Offers, Tourism, Events and existing Property under one shared location selector. Content availability can grow area by area; the selector and category model must not be hardcoded to one neighbourhood. Admins confirm submitted non-property content before publication. Posters create and maintain their own entries and validity dates. Offers expire at their end time; events leave upcoming results after their end or publication cutoff; Tourism follows its explicitly chosen availability rule. Eligibility is enforced when content is read, even if a job fails. Release 2: a small Home Services pilot. ## Pilot scorecard and decision gates

Segment every measure by category and first supplied place; keep Property as its own segment so new traffic cannot hide a decline. Store event definitions and query links with the scorecard, remove test traffic, and record missing instrumentation rather than filling gaps with assumptions.

| Measure | Numerator / denominator or unit | Cadence and interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Useful supply | Daily count of simultaneously eligible, admin-confirmed entries and distinct active posters per category/place; also count places with at least one eligible result. | Daily snapshot. A catalogue total is not live supply. |
| Discovery with results | Sessions that selected a place/category and received at least one eligible result / sessions that selected that same place/category. Track detail opens / result sessions separately. | Weekly by source, category and place; exclude requests cancelled by a newer selection. |
| Shopping Offer intent | Unique saves, directions or code-copy actions / eligible Offer detail sessions. Record action type separately. | Weekly. Redemption requires a distinct customer-reported or poster-confirmed source and should never be inferred from a click. |
| Tourism and Event intent | Unique enquiry/booking/ticket-link actions / eligible detail sessions, by type. | Weekly. Verified bookings or attendance need separate confirmation. |
| Property outcomes | New enquiries, booked viewings, applications and buyer interests / eligible Property detail sessions, each as its own rate and count. | Weekly against the dated Property baseline and comparable source/place mix. |
| Repeat use | Identified or privacy-safe returning visitor cohort within 7 or 30 days / visitors in the corresponding fully matured first-visit cohort. | Weekly for mature 7-day cohorts; monthly only after 30-day cohorts mature. State the identity/deduplication rule. |
| Poster upkeep and value | Posters who corrected, renewed or confirmed continued accuracy within their natural content cycle / posters whose content reached that cycle; collect reported outcomes separately. | Review after each category's relevant cycle. An event ending is not a failed renewal. |
| Reliability and safety | Failed visitor actions, stale/incorrect eligible results, notification failures and unresolved reports; report count, affected sessions where measurable, and median/max resolution time. | Daily operations review and weekly trend. Any cross-poster leak or actionable expired content stops promotion. |
| Operating cost | Reviewer minutes per submitted version, total admin hours per week and open queue age, against assigned capacity. | Daily queue check, weekly capacity review. |

Before 6.1, Product and Operations must fill a threshold sheet for every metric used to decide continuation: **target value, minimum acceptable value, denominator, minimum sample or content cycle, observation window, data source, owner and decision date**. Values are deliberately **TBD** until the poster cohort and capacity are known; no commercial target in this document is a validated fact. Record the decision as **continue**, **repair and remeasure**, or **stop/pause**, with the evidence and owner. Technical gates are non-negotiable: no unauthorized access, no actionable expired/withdrawn content, intact core Property journeys, functioning measurement and a rehearsed fallback.
