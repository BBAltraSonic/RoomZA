Identify opportunities to add moments of joy, personality, and unexpected polish that transform functional interfaces into delightful experiences.

## MANDATORY PREPARATION

Gather context: target audience, brand personality (playful vs professional vs quirky vs elegant), and what's appropriate for the domain. If unclear, ask the user directly.

**CRITICAL**: Delight should enhance usability, never obscure it.

## Assess Delight Opportunities

1. **Find natural delight moments**:
   - **Success states**: Completed actions
   - **Empty states**: First-time experiences
   - **Loading states**: Waiting periods
   - **Achievements**: Milestones, streaks
   - **Interactions**: Hover states, clicks
   - **Easter eggs**: Hidden discoveries

2. **Define delight strategy**:
   - **Subtle sophistication**: Refined micro-interactions (luxury brands)
   - **Playful personality**: Whimsical illustrations and copy (consumer apps)
   - **Helpful surprises**: Anticipating needs (productivity tools)

## Delight Principles

- Delight moments should be quick (< 1 second)
- Never delay core functionality for delight
- Make delight skippable or subtle
- Match delight to emotional moment
- Vary responses (not same animation every time)

## Delight Techniques

### Micro-interactions & Animation
- Satisfying button press feedback
- Playful loading animations
- Success animations (checkmark draw, confetti for achievements)
- Hover surprises (icons that animate, color shifts)

### Personality in Copy
- Playful error messages (match brand tone)
- Encouraging empty states
- Playful labels & tooltips (when appropriate)

### Satisfying Interactions
- Drag and drop with lift effect and snap animation
- Smooth toggle switches with spring physics
- Progress celebrations at 100%
- Form fields that animate on focus

### Easter Eggs & Hidden Delights
- Konami code unlocks special theme
- Console messages for developers
- Seasonal touches (subtle, tasteful)
- Time-of-day variations

### Loading & Waiting States
- Interesting rotating loading messages
- Fun facts or tips while waiting
- Progress bars with personality

### Celebration Moments
- Confetti for major milestones
- Animated checkmarks for completions
- Personalized messages ("You published your 10th article!")

**NEVER**:
- Delay core functionality for delight
- Force users through delightful moments
- Use delight to hide poor UX
- Sacrifice performance for delight
- Be inappropriate for context
- Ignore accessibility

## Verify Delight Quality

- **Doesn't annoy**: Still pleasant after 100th time?
- **Doesn't block**: Can users opt out or skip?
- **Performant**: No jank, no slowdown
- **Appropriate**: Matches brand and context
- **Accessible**: Works with reduced motion, screen readers
