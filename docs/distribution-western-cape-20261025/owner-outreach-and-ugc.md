# Owner invitations and genuine UGC

These are individual message drafts. Replace bracketed fields with facts before sending. No messages have been sent.

## First invitation

Hi [name]. I am preparing Pinpoint's Western Cape rooms and affordable-rentals launch. I found your [public business page / permissioned introduction] for [town or suburb]. Are you the right person to speak to about featuring a room you manage, when it is available? I would first confirm the details and permissions with you. There is no promise of enquiries or tenants.

Use a relevant public business contact route or an existing relationship. Do not collect group-member contact lists. The supplied shortlist is a starting point for fit checks, not a contact list with consent.

## When an owner is interested

Thanks. Which town and suburb is the room in, what is the monthly rent and deposit, what is included, and when is it available? Is it a private room or a shared sleeping space? Please also confirm you own or manage the room and can authorise the listing and its photos. Once the listing flow is ready, we can use Pinpoint's normal submission and review process.

Record an owner once using a neutral code such as OWN-001. Do not put identity documents, bank details or tenant applications in this marketing tracker.

## Footage request

Could you film five short clips of the actual accommodation in daylight? Hold your phone vertically and move slowly. Please show (1) the entrance without revealing access codes or a precise private address, (2) the room and storage, (3) the kitchen, (4) the bathroom, and (5) a shared area. Each clip can be 5–10 seconds. Keep people, identity documents, keys and private details out of view. Send clean originals without music or social-platform watermarks. We will confirm the facts and your permission before posting.

## One follow-up, if appropriate

Hi [name], following up once on the Pinpoint room-feature invitation for [town/suburb]. If it is relevant, I can send the short listing checklist. If the timing does not suit you, no problem and I will leave it there.

Leave at least five days after the first invitation. Stop on a decline or opt-out. No reply is not permission to publish or reuse footage.

## Owner intake record

| Field | Required fact |
|---|---|
| Owner code | Stable neutral ID; one code per owner/business |
| Authority | Confirmation that they own/manage the accommodation and may authorise its listing/media |
| Town and suburb | Actual property location; do not invent precise coordinates |
| Accommodation | Private room, shared room or other accurate type |
| Monthly amount | Confirm exact rent and whether it is per room or per person |
| Deposit and upfront costs | Confirm separately from monthly rent |
| Included and extra costs | Utilities, internet, parking and any stated charges |
| Available date | Owner-confirmed date and date last checked |
| Facilities | What is private and what is shared |
| Media | File names, current filming date, rights holder, and organic permission record |
| Listing | Actual submitted/published listing ID and share URL, when available |
| Public contact | Use the application's existing consent process; do not infer consent from a marketing reply |

## Organic media permission template

Ask for an explicit written response that identifies the assets and scope:

I confirm that I can authorise use of [specific file names] showing [town/suburb and room identifier]. I permit Pinpoint to crop, caption and edit these files without changing the accommodation facts, and to publish the resulting organic posts on its Facebook, Instagram and TikTok accounts for 90 days from the first publication. This does not permit paid advertising. Please use [business name / no personal attribution]. The facts above are accurate as of [date]. Contact me at our existing correspondence channel for corrections or removal from Pinpoint-controlled posts.

Retain the owner's actual response and any conditions in a restricted folder, linked by neutral evidence code. If the owner changes the scope, use their agreed scope. Do not mark a permission granted merely because this template was sent. Treat this as an operational record, not a claim of legal certification.

## Separate paid-use permission

Request only after organic participation and the launch checks:

For [specific final video IDs], may Pinpoint additionally use the approved edits in Facebook and Instagram paid ads for a 30-day period, shown in the Western Cape? Please confirm the permitted dates and whether your business may be credited. This permission would not authorise ads from your personal or creator account. Any creator-identity advertising would require separate platform authorisation.

Record Yes/No, permitted platforms, start/end dates and the evidence code. Organic permission never automatically becomes paid permission. Use the current platform disclosure and identity permissions where required.

## Genuine walkthrough replacement: 25 seconds

| Time | Picture | On-screen words |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Actual room | “[Town/suburb] • [private/shared] room” |
| 3–8s | Room and storage | “R[confirmed rent]/month [per room/per person]” |
| 8–13s | Kitchen and bathroom | “[Confirmed private/shared facilities]” |
| 13–19s | Actual room or simple facts card | “Deposit R[amount] • [included costs] • Available [date]” |
| 19–25s | Room plus final card | “See this room on Pinpoint • [tested destination instruction]” |

Before publication: replace every bracket, reconfirm availability that day, confirm normal listing review/publication, verify the room link, record organic permission and check the crop. If any fact or permission is missing, publish the supplied V05 educational fallback instead.

After publication, send the owner their final post link and ask: “Would you like to share this feature with your own audience?” Their sharing is voluntary. Do not imply an existing tenant found accommodation through Pinpoint unless that person genuinely did and approved the account.
