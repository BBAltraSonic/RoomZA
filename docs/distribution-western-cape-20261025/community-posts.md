# Eight useful community contributions

Use one on Wednesday and one on Sunday each week. Adapt to the actual question and group rules. Add a Pinpoint signature only where promotion is allowed. These are drafts, not published posts.

## Week 1 Wednesday: full monthly cost

When comparing rooms in the Western Cape, I find it useful to write down the rent and then ask which costs are extra: electricity, water, Wi-Fi and transport from the specific suburb. Two rooms with the same advertised rent can have different monthly totals. What extra cost do you usually ask about first?

## Week 1 Sunday: owner preparation

For anyone preparing a room advert: naming the town and suburb, rent, deposit, included costs and available date makes the first conversation much clearer. Current photos of the actual room and shared spaces are useful too. What detail do you wish more room adverts included?

## Week 2 Wednesday: before travelling

Before travelling to a room viewing, ask whether that exact room is still available for your move-in date, what the total move-in amount is and which costs are extra each month. It is a short checklist, but it can help you plan which rooms to view.

## Week 2 Sunday: public questions

If you ask about a room publicly, naming the area, your intended move-in date and the kind of room you need gives people useful context. Keep identity documents and private contact details out of public comments, and use the owner's appropriate contact route for a viewing.

## Week 3 Wednesday: walkthrough checklist

A useful room walkthrough should show the actual room as well as the kitchen, bathroom and any spaces you would share. Ask whether the footage is current and what is private versus shared. What part of a room would you want to see before arranging a viewing?

## Week 3 Sunday: owner filming tip

If you are filming a room for an advert, try five short vertical clips in daylight: entrance, room, kitchen, bathroom and shared space. Move slowly and keep private documents, access codes and people out of view. Add the suburb, costs and available date alongside the footage.

## Week 4 Wednesday: compare consistently

One way to compare rooms is to use the same checklist for each: suburb, full monthly cost, move-in cost, available date, shared spaces, house rules and commute. That makes the next questions easier to spot before you choose what to view.

## Week 4 Sunday: keep adverts current

Room owners: if a room is taken or its available date changes, update the advert as soon as you can. The same applies to rent, included costs and photos. Renters can ask more relevant questions when the details are current.
