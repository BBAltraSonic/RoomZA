## Campaign rules

Use Pinpoint consistently. Keep Western Cape as the campaign region; every real property feature must name its town/suburb and actual costs. Regional education posts need no invented property location or price. Do not promise verified listings, protected payments, guaranteed accommodation, guaranteed leads, free listing terms or response times unless the product and business terms support the statement.

Preparation CTA: **Follow Pinpoint for Western Cape rental updates.** Owner CTA: **Follow Pinpoint for room-listing preparation tips.** Change to a specific browse or submit CTA only after the relevant live journey works. Do not promote area alerts before testing actual delivery and opt-out. There is no new general waitlist in this kit.

The supplied videos deliberately work without audio. Optional scripts and SRT files are provided for a human voiceover. Music is optional; use audio cleared for each destination and avoid assuming a platform library permits reuse on another platform. Export the clean master without social-platform watermarks.
