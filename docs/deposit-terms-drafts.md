## Terms of Service

Replace the current "Fees" section with these two sections.

### Fees

Pinpoint does not charge renters or landlords to list, search, apply, message or book viewings. The only charges are on rental deposits paid through Pinpoint, described in the next section and in the Payments and Refunds policy.

### Deposits paid through Pinpoint

When a landlord publishes a deposit on a listing and adds bank details, a renter whose application is approved can pay the deposit through Pinpoint. Payments are processed by Paystack. Pinpoint holds the deposit until the renter moves in.

- **The renter moves in.** When the renter confirms they moved in, Pinpoint keeps a commission of 10% of the deposit and pays the rest to the landlord by bank transfer. If the renter does not respond, the same happens 72 hours after the move-in date starts, or 72 hours after payment if the renter paid after that.
- **The renter cancels before the move-in date.** The deposit is refunded less a cancellation fee equal to the processing fee Paystack charged on the payment. The renter sees an estimate of this fee before paying and the exact amount after paying.
- **The renter is not satisfied on or after the move-in date.** The landlord has 24 hours to say the renter moved in. If the landlord does not respond, the deposit is refunded less the cancellation fee. If the landlord responds, Pinpoint reviews what happened and decides whether the deposit goes to the landlord or back to the renter. When Pinpoint refunds the renter after a review, it may waive the cancellation fee.
- **The landlord cancels or withdraws the approval.** The deposit is refunded in full, with no cancellation fee.

**The landlord's obligation.** A landlord who receives a deposit through Pinpoint receives it less Pinpoint's commission, but remains responsible to the renter for the full deposit under the lease and the law, including returning it at the end of the lease. The commission is a fee the landlord pays Pinpoint and does not reduce what the landlord owes the renter.

**Pinpoint's decisions.** When Pinpoint decides where a held deposit goes, it decides only what happens to the money it holds. The decision does not settle any other claim between the renter and the landlord under the lease.

**Bank details.** The landlord must keep the bank details for payouts accurate. Pinpoint may delay a payout and contact the landlord if the details change after the renter has paid.

## Payments and Refunds

Replace the whole current body with this.

### What Pinpoint charges

Pinpoint does not charge for listing, searching, applying, messaging or viewings, and offers no subscriptions or paid listings.

On a rental deposit paid through Pinpoint:

| What happens | Renter | Landlord | Pinpoint |
|---|---|---|---|
| The renter moves in | Pays the deposit | Receives the deposit less a 10% commission | Keeps the commission |
| The renter cancels | Gets the deposit back less the cancellation fee | Receives nothing | Keeps the cancellation fee |
| The landlord cancels | Gets the full deposit back | Receives nothing | Keeps nothing |

The cancellation fee equals the processing fee Paystack charged on that payment, which Paystack does not return when a payment is refunded. Before paying, the renter sees an estimate of the fee; after paying, the exact amount. If Paystack does not report the fee for a payment, no cancellation fee is kept on it.

### How refunds work

Refunds go back to the card or account the renter paid with, through Paystack, and can take several working days to show. If Paystack cannot make a refund, Pinpoint contacts the renter and refunds them by bank transfer.

### Disputes

If a renter says they are not satisfied and the landlord says the renter moved in, Pinpoint reviews what happened and tells both people its decision. Pinpoint may ask either person for evidence, such as messages, photos or the lease.

### Contact

Questions about a deposit can be sent to [support@pinpoint.co.za](mailto:support@pinpoint.co.za). Include the listing and the date you paid.
